# Solution Definition — Women’s Beauty Salon Booking

## Solution thesis

Create a digital self-service booking and appointment-management experience for a single women’s beauty salon that brings services, specialists, pricing/duration, and valid availability into one coherent customer journey.

The product should let customers move from discovery to a confirmed appointment with less manual coordination while preserving the salon’s control over service eligibility, staff schedules, duration, and appointment rules.

## Core product promise

### Customer

Find the right beauty service, choose the right specialist, and book a valid appointment with confidence.

### Salon

Reduce repetitive booking coordination without losing operational control over services, specialists, schedules, and appointment rules.

## Problem → solution mapping

| Problem | Solution direction |
| --- | --- |
| Service information is fragmented | Structured service discovery |
| Specialist suitability is unclear | Clear service ↔ specialist relationships and specialist profiles |
| Pricing and duration are uncertain | Visible service pricing model and expected duration |
| Availability is hidden | Valid bookable time-slot discovery |
| Booking requires repeated messages | Self-service booking flow |
| Appointment changes require staff contact | Customer appointment management |
| Rules live mainly in staff knowledge | Explicit product-enforced booking constraints |

## Core capabilities

### 1. Service Discovery

Help customers understand what the salon offers, what each service is for, expected price or pricing model, expected duration, and which specialists can provide it.

### 2. Specialist Discovery

Help customers understand relevant specialists through meaningful information such as expertise, supported services, short profile information, portfolio/work examples where useful, and related availability.

A specialist is part of the customer’s trust and decision process, not only a scheduling resource.

### 3. Availability & Scheduling

Only show times that can actually accommodate the selected service with an eligible specialist for the full required duration while respecting working schedules and existing appointments.

### 4. Booking

Turn the customer’s selected service, specialist preference, valid date/time, and required customer details into a clear confirmed appointment.

Typical flow:

`Service → Specialist / Any eligible specialist → Date & Time → Customer details → Review → Confirm`

### 5. Customer Appointment Management

Allow customers to understand and manage upcoming appointments without unnecessary staff contact, including viewing details and, when policy allows, cancelling or rescheduling.

### 6. Salon Appointment Operations

Give salon staff the minimum operational controls required to supervise appointments and preserve valid scheduling behavior without turning the product into a complete salon ERP.

## Must solve

- clear service discovery
- relevant specialist discovery
- pricing/pricing-model visibility
- service duration visibility
- valid availability discovery
- service + specialist + time selection
- confirmed appointment creation
- upcoming appointment visibility
- permitted cancellation/rescheduling
- prevention of invalid bookings
- essential salon-side appointment operations

## Product boundary

This product is a booking and appointment-management system for a single women’s beauty salon.

It is **not** a complete salon-management ERP. Its purpose is to improve the customer-to-appointment journey and the salon operations directly required to support that journey.
